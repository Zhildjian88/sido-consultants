What We Do

SiDO Consultants supports founders, executives, and leadership teams by designing analytics-informed frameworks that improve oversight, accountability, and decision quality.

Our work includes:

  • Risk and decision analytics
  • Fraud and anomaly analytics (sector-neutral)
  • Operational and control analytics
  • AI governance and oversight frameworks
  • Executive decision support using data and analytics

How We Work

We engage during early-stage, transition, or high-uncertainty phases where decisions are front-loaded and difficult to reverse.

Our engagements are:

  • Advisory-led
  • Analytics-driven
  • Time-bound
  • Non-statutory and non-executive
We do not act as management, auditors, regulators, or compliance officers.

Areas of Focus

  • Data-driven risk and control design

  • Transaction and operational risk analytics
  • AI governance and oversight design
  • Model risk awareness and decision accountability
  • Governance and reporting frameworks
  • Cross-functional alignment between data, risk, and leadership

AI Governance

We support organisations in establishing practical governance for AI-enabled and data-driven systems.

This includes defining accountability, oversight, decision boundaries, and risk awareness around the use of analytical and AI-supported models.

Our focus is on governance design and decision control, not model development or regulatory representation.

About

SiDO Consultants is an independent advisory firm specialising in data analytics, risk decision-making, and AI governance.

We work with founders, executives, and leadership teams to design decision frameworks and governance structures that improve oversight and accountability in complex, high-stakes environments.

Our approach is analytics-driven, time-bound, and focused on real-world decision problems rather than generic reporting or technology implementation.
